Accountability Standing & Proportionality Policy

Standardized Governance Framework for Community Standing, Demerit Depreciation & Disciplinary Protocols

Principle 1

Non-Linear Demerit Proportionality

Demerit points are not directly 1:1 proportional to appreciation points. Earning 100 appreciation points and subsequently receiving 5 demerit points does not result in a simple net score of 95 points.

Instead, demerit points carry variable proportionality based on the operational importance and seriousness of the assigned responsibility. For critical assignments or high-impact commitments, 1 demerit point may correspond to a deduction of up to 5 appreciation points.

Principle 2

Progressive Multiplier Tiers

As negative points accumulate, the system automatically escalates the depreciation frequency across distinct penalty tiers:

Penalty Tier Cumulative Negative Points Depreciation Multiplier Net Deductive Impact Standing Status
Tier 1 (Base) 1 to 10 points 2x Multiplier 1 demerit reduces 2 appreciation points Normal Active
Tier 2 (Elevated) 11 to 15 points 3x Multiplier 1 demerit reduces 3 appreciation points Advisory Watch
Tier 3 (Critical) 16 to 31 points 4x+ Multiplier 1 demerit reduces 4+ appreciation points (accelerated) Strict Probation
Tier 4 (Actionable) 32+ points Disciplinary Review Full standing suspension Subject to Termination
Principle 3

32-Point Disciplinary Review & Potential Termination

Any member who accumulates a cumulative total of 32 or more negative points is immediately placed under Administrative Conduct Review.

Continuous accumulation of non-disciplinary points reflects repeated unfulfilled responsibilities or unaddressed procedural violations, and may lead to formal termination and revocation of community privileges, badges, and committee tenure.

Principle 4

Mandatory Daily Tracker Reporting & Advance Leave Protocol

Active visibility is foundational to peer leadership. Daily tracker updates are strictly mandatory for all active team members:

  • Daily Lead Briefing: You must inform your assigned team lead of your activities for that day — even if you had zero output on that specific day. Transparent communication is always respected.
  • Advance Notice for Unavailability: If you anticipate being unavailable or taking leave for 2 to 3 consecutive days, you are required to inform your team lead in advance so the tracker can be officially updated with an excused mark.
  • Automated Negative Marking: If your tracker entry remains blank or unreported for any working day without verified prior notice, an automated system-generated negative marking is registered against your accountability record.
Principle 5

Grievance Redressal & Direct Personal Appeal

InteractUp maintains a 100% fair, transparent, and empathetic community culture. You always have the right of appeal:

If you believe any system demerit point was assigned in error, due to a technical glitch, or without proper context, you can directly and personally reach out to the Admin, your President, or Secretary.

Your concern will be evaluated objectively during executive audit sessions, and if verified, the negative point will be formally revoked and all depreciated points restored to your profile immediately.
